Short-term courses · Ayurveda

Ayurveda

A complete science of living: body, food, seasons, habits and health, understood together.

These short-term courses build clear, practical awareness of Ayurveda from authentic source texts, with everyday life at the centre. The focus is not professional practice but informed living, conscious choices and whole wellbeing.

They suit anyone who wants a deeper understanding of lifestyle, food and health from the Ayurvedic view. Each course can be taken on its own and is taught online.

Choose your course

Āyurvedīya Jīvana Paddhati

Introduction to the Ayurvedic lifestyle

Ayurveda as a practical life science rooted in daily living. Learn how daily routines, seasonal practices and your own constitution shape long-term health. For anyone seeking a balanced, mindful and sustainable way of life.

Āyurvedīya Āhāra Paddhati

The science of food in Ayurveda

Food, digestion and eating habits through the Ayurvedic lens: gut health, food combinations, seasonal diets and common mistakes, drawn from classical texts and lived Indian wisdom. It brings simplicity and clarity back to how we eat.

Āyurvedīya Svāsthya Prajñā

Health and disease in Ayurveda

Foundational awareness of Ayurveda as a medical system, from a consumer's point of view: how Ayurveda sees diagnosis, common diseases, home remedies and simple household therapies, without clinical complexity. For informed health choices and better use of Ayurvedic care.
