Artists and scholars

Artist Repository

Documenting the journeys of artists who carry India's living traditions with care, and honouring their teachers.

Our community is growing with dancers, musicians, storytellers, scholars and seekers who carry India's living traditions with care. This repository documents their journeys, honours their teachers, and helps rasikas, organisers and students discover authentic artists.

If you are an artist connected to the classical, folk, yogic or scriptural arts, you can submit your profile. Your details help build a reliable archive that stays true to the spirit of Brahmavidya.

Submit your profile

Thank you for contributing to this growing repository of art, lineage and learning.
