A PageMaker book from 1998, an InDesign file from the press, a Word manuscript in Nudi: each opens with its pages, frames and styles in place, and you choose what happens to every old font before anything changes.

PageMaker, as you remember it

The same nine menus, the fourteen-tool toolbox, the control palette, windowshade handles on text blocks, master pages, Autoflow, Story Editor and PageMaker’s keyboard shortcuts. People who set books in PageMaker for twenty years start working at once.

  • Smart open: every old font is surveyed and a Unicode font and size suggested, measured on your own text so lines break where they did
  • Text blocks start with exactly the text they had in PageMaker
  • Save back to PageMaker 7 when the press still needs it
ಕ

Kannada keyboards built in

Nudi KGP and phonetic Kannada, phonetic Devanagari and IAST. Scroll Lock switches, as in Nudi.

ॐ

Sanskrit tools

Śloka and IAST styles, transliteration between Kannada, Devanagari and IAST in one keystroke, Vedic signs in the Glyphs palette.

Tab

Publications in tabs

Open several files at once. Ctrl+Tab moves between them; Ctrl+Shift+T brings back the one you closed.

¶

Reflow and fit

Overset text flows onto new pages in the page’s columns. Fit Frame to Text, Balance Columns, text wrap.

.*

Find and Change

Across bold and italic, whole words in Kannada and Devanagari, regular expressions, Find Font.

†

Footnotes, index, contents

Footnotes numbered in Kannada or Devanagari digits, an index, a table of contents, running heads.

Snap

Smart Guides

Objects snap to the edges and centres of other objects while you drag, as in InDesign.

✓

Live preflight

A light in the status bar counts missing fonts, missing pictures and overset text before the press does.

⊞

From Scribus, Word and LibreOffice

Pages and Outline palettes, Align and Distribute, tables, gradients, a Library, Data Merge, Format Painter, typographer’s quotes, sticky notes for proof comments, a watermark and page border, Read Aloud.

✦

Your own name on it

Tell BV Mudrana your name when you install it. The start screen says Namaste, and the title bar reads as your own Mudrana.

Four ways to work, seven looks

Choose the interface that fits the person at the keyboard. Every mode has every command; only the way in changes.

Fonts made at Brahmavidya, included

Each one keeps the letterforms of a font people already printed with, now as Unicode. BV Mudrana installs them in Windows for your account, so Word and every other program can use them too. Click a line and type your own text.

How it grew

Each version installs over the one before with a small update file. Nothing has to be uninstalled.

  1. 0.9

    Publications in tabs, a new start screen, InDesign IDML and INDD, PageMaker 5, Chanakya and Nalanda to Unicode in BV Chitkala and BV Nanda, Smart Guides, live preflight, BV Matanga.

  2. 0.8

    Reflow overset text, Find and Change with regular expressions, Find Font, Format Painter, fields and special characters, watermarks, Read Aloud, your name on the title bar.

  3. 0.7

    Praja (Prajavani) fonts: Praja Unicode, Praja01 to Praja08, Praja documents in Word and RTF, and updates that install over the old version.

  4. 0.6

    Four interface modes and seven themes, from Material You to Classic PageMaker 7.

  5. 0.5

    Smart open with measured font sizes, Kannada keyboards, footnotes, more PageMaker formatting kept.

  6. 0.4

    PageMaker’s menus drawn in the window, Pages, Outline, Align and Glyphs palettes, tables from Scribus.

  7. 0.3

    Save as PageMaker 7 and 6.5, the Brahmavidya Kannada converter everywhere.

  8. 0.2

    Opens PageMaker 6.5 and 7 files and turns Nudi text into Unicode Kannada.
