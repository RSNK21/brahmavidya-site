Our inspiration

Mahāmahopādhyāya Padmaśrī Dr. R. Sathyanarayana

Musical inheritance and cultural background of the scholar who shaped Brahmavidya.

Dr. R. Sathyanarayana (born 9 May 1927, Mysuru), M.Sc. (Chemistry), Ph.D., D.Litt., D.Litt. (Honoris Causa), was an internationally acclaimed musicologist and danceologist. His life and work brought together deep scholarship, a rich musical inheritance and service to the Indian classical arts.

He belonged to the lineage of the saint-composer Śrī Tyāgarāja and came from a family steeped in music, scholarship and cultural tradition.

A lineage of luminaries

  • Paternal grandfatherPallavi Ramalingayya, famed for extempore pallavis in 108 Desi Tālas; trained under Manambuchavadi Venkatasubbayya, a senior disciple of Tyāgarāja.
  • FatherB. Ramaiah, a renowned musician.
  • MotherSmt. Varalakshmi, foremost disciple of Veena Sundarashastri of the Mysore royal court. She initiated him into music at the age of five.
  • FamilyHis siblings, wife, children, in-laws and grandchildren are noted musicians and dancers; his daughter-in-law is a Bharatanatyam dancer of repute.

Teaching

  • Chemistry (1949–1984)
  • Karnataka music (1945–1984)
  • Musicology and danceology (from 1951 onwards)

Academic distinctions

  • First interdisciplinary D.Litt. from the University of Mysore
  • Golden Jubilee Awards for research in Fine Arts (three times)
  • Certificate of Honour, International Biographical Centre, Cambridge
  • Highest Award for Lecture-Demonstration, Music Academy, Madras

Cultural delegations

  • One-man cultural mission to Europe and the UK (Government of India)
  • Led delegations to the USSR, China, Malaysia and Singapore
  • Key participant in seminars in London, Tashkent, Samarkand, Moscow and Delhi

Awards and honours (selected)

  • Padma Shri, Government of India (2018)
  • Rajya Sangita Vidwan Award, Government of Karnataka (2011)
  • Bharatamuni Samman, Nalanda Research Centre, Mumbai (2012)
  • Sangeet Natak Akademi Fellowship (Akademi Ratna)
  • Musicologist Award, Madras Music Academy
  • Karnataka Rajyotsava Award

Honorific titles

  • Mahāmahopādhyāya
  • Rāṣṭrabhūṣaṇa
  • Saṅgīta Kalā Ratna
  • Saṅgīta Sudhākara
  • Gānaśāstra Vidyāvāridhi
  • Saṅgīta Śāstra Śiromaṇi
  • Abhinava Bharatācārya

His birthday, 9 May, is celebrated as Indian Musicology Day.
