Yoga

A disciplined inward journey, supported by precise physical, breath-based and contemplative practice.

Rooted in Yoga Śāstra, these courses restore depth and clarity to modern Yoga learning by balancing philosophy with lived practice.

Each course addresses a distinct dimension of Yoga. Choose one focused path, or take all three for a well-rounded understanding of body, breath and mind.
