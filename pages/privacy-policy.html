Privacy Policy

Effective 28 March 2025 · Last updated 7 November 2026

At Brahmavidya (brahmavidya.co.in) we value your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number and similar details when you write to us, apply for a course, submit an artist profile or subscribe to our letters.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical data such as browser type, device, IP address and pages visited, for security and to keep the site working.

2. Services that handle your information for us

  • Newsletter: when you subscribe, your email address is held by WordPress.com (Automattic), which sends our letters. Every letter has a link to unsubscribe.
  • Course applications: applications are made through Google Forms, operated by Google.
  • Videos: YouTube videos load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Downloads and tools: some software downloads are served from GitHub. The Kannada converters on this site run entirely in your browser; the text you paste is not sent to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and handle course applications.
  • To send our letters, if you asked for them.
  • To improve the website and keep it secure.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share it only with the services listed above, which help us run the website, and only as needed.

5. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Your browser may store small preferences locally. Third-party services such as YouTube or Google Forms may set their own cookies when you use them.

6. Third-party links

Our site links to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical care to protect your information.

8. Your rights

You may contact us at any time to ask for a copy of your personal data, to correct or delete it, or to withdraw your consent.
