Dr. R. S. Nandakumar

Music, musicology and Indology

Dr. R. S. Nandakumar is a natural musician and the son and disciple of Mahāmahopādhyāya Dr. R. Sathyanarayana, the internationally acclaimed scholar and musicologist.

His music is marked by a rich, rangy and supple voice built through years of practice, a wide and diverse repertoire, and a pioneering revival of the ancient song traditions of Indian music, some more than a thousand years old. He presents these treasures in contemporary settings for today's listeners.

He was the first artist to perform the Tirupati Tirumala Śilālekha Suḷādis at their lokārpaṇa, after the publication of the Tirupati Tirumala inscriptions by TTD. He has contributed to UNESCO's intangible cultural heritage projects in Karnataka. His doctoral work on the musical works and life of Śrīpādarāja, which traces the history of Karnataka music, earned him the D.Litt from Kannada University, Hampi.

He has toured Europe, South Asia and America, appears often on television and radio, and has performed on major stages in Karnataka, India and abroad. The Karnataka Sangeeta Nritya Academy honoured him with the title Karnataka Kalāśrī.

Dr. Radhika Nandakumar

Head of Dance, Bharatanatyam

Dr. Radhika Nandakumar is among the contemporary pioneers of the celebrated Mysore school of Bharatanatyam. Her style unites spatial precision with melodic depth, reaching toward aesthetic divinity through classical form.

Drawing on art historiography, her research has refined the Mysore tradition with artistic and intellectual clarity. She has created new expressions rooted in contemporary life while staying grounded in the śāstra and the gurukula way of learning.

Her work balances textual tradition and embodied knowledge. Through careful restoration and faithful reinterpretation of classical texts, she gives Bharatanatyam new life, drawing on the past to speak to the present and the future. Her choreographies are known for being authentic, vibrant and deeply evocative.

Dr. M. V. Prashanth

Ayurveda

Dr. Prashanth is an Ayurvedic practitioner and the founder of Ayurdhaama, an Ayurvedic centre in Bengaluru. With over two decades of experience, he offers authentic Ayurvedic consultation, treatment and Pañcakarma therapy, and works to make Ayurveda accessible, affordable and effective in India and abroad.

He has consulted at Chakreshwari Ayurveda Chikitsalaya and Amrutham Traditional Ayurvedic Treatment Centre, is known for his expertise in nāḍī parīkṣā (pulse diagnosis), and is the author of Ārogyamaya Jīvana.

He holds a B.A.M.S. (1997) and an M.D. in Dravyaguṇa, with further training in Reiki, Hologram Healing, Acupressure and Marma Cikitsā. He has been a visiting consultant at many Ayurvedic and medical centres and an adviser to academic bodies including Sakshi and Brahmavidya.

Dr. Prashanth was initiated into the Śrīvidyā Tantra Mārga by Padma Shri Mahāmahopādhyāya Dr. R. Sathyanarayana. He teaches Ayurveda widely, appears on television and social media, and is a Fellow of the International Council of Ayurveda, Academy of General Education, Manipal, and a member of the Creative Life Project of Sakshi, Bengaluru.

Sri Vijayasarathi

Yoga and Śrīvidyā

Sri Vijayasarathi is a scholar and spiritual practitioner, adept in Yoga and an initiated Śrīvidyā sādhaka, blessed by his guru Śrī Satyānandanātha to walk the path of liberation taught in the Śākta doctrine.

He speaks with clarity and depth on Yoga, Śrīvidyā and dhārmic living, and inspires many through a life rooted in the values and practices of Sanātana Dharma. He brings a spiritual and philosophical dimension to the pursuit of knowledge and self-realisation at Brahmavidya.
