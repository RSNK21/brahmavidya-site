Artist Repository

Our community is growing with dancers, musicians, storytellers, scholars, and seekers who carry India’s living traditions with care. This space is created to document their journeys, honour their teachers, and make it easier for rasikas, organisers, and students to discover authentic artists.

If you are an artist connected to classical, folk, yogic, or scriptural arts, you can submit your profile here. Your details will help build a reliable archive that stays true to the spirit of Brahmavidya.
